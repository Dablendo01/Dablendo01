LANTERN & DUST — STANDING DIRECTIVES (apply to every new story from now on)
Add these to the top of the Claude Project's custom instructions. Last set: anti-slop / grounded-prose rule (2026-08-11).

1. LEAD WITH WOMEN'S HUMILIATION.
   Default every new story to this engine: a sympathetic woman is publicly
   humiliated / mocked / cast out -> she endures with dignity -> a hidden truth
   or a hidden protector -> a public reversal that vindicates her in front of the
   people who shamed her. She is the underdog we root FOR — NEVER the cruel woman
   who "gets taught a lesson."
   Pull premises and titles from humiliation-title-bank.txt (bent from proven
   African-tales outliers, the same engine at larger scale). Vary the humiliation
   setting (auction, church, barn dance, market, wedding, courthouse steps, the
   servant's table) so stories don't clone each other. Check canon-registry before
   naming anyone or anywhere.

2. THUMBNAILS: NO TEXT, MAXIMUM DRAMA + CLARITY.
   Never put overlay text on a thumbnail. Every thumbnail must be ONE instantly
   readable, dramatic emotional moment that makes a scrolling viewer need to click
   and then stay:
   - A large, well-lit human FACE at its emotional peak (shame, defiance, stunned
     recognition, a mother's fury). The face is the click.
   - One clear action/story beat, nothing cluttered — readable at 168px wide.
   - Warm-vs-cold light contrast (lantern/gold against cold rain/dusk/snow),
     burnt-sienna & gold grade, photorealistic, 16:9.
   - The antagonist or jeering crowd may appear, but must never steal focus from
     the woman's face.
   - Test at thumbnail size: if you can't read the emotion in half a second, simplify.

3. WRITE GROUNDED, HUMAN PROSE — STRIP AI-CADENCE TELLS. (anti-slop; every spoken script)
   An outside editor flagged our scripts for generated-fiction cadence. From now on, in the
   SPOKEN narration:
   A. NO conversational meta-phrases / narrator asides. Cut: "friend", "stay by the fire" /
      "stay a while by our fire" (as a spoken line), "think on that (a moment)", "I wish you
      could have seen...", "you see", "if you want the root of it", "mind you". The narrator
      stays warm through concrete DETAIL and restraint, not by talking at the viewer.
      >>> The "stay a while by our fire" tagline LIVES ON — but ONLY in the video DESCRIPTION
          and PINNED COMMENT. Never spoken in the VO.
   B. NO predictable tricolon / rule-of-three lists. Break the "X. Y. Z." three-beat pattern
      (e.g. "Every acre. Every head. Every dollar."; "No tears. No flinch. Nothing to feed
      on."). Use two beats, or four, or fold them into one flowing clause — vary the count.
   C. NO AI-typical false-contrast setups — the "It wasn't X, it was Y", "She could have done
      A, B, or C. She didn't", "not because... but because..." antithesis template. At most
      once per script, if at all; otherwise state it plainly.
   D. NO cliché cadence tells: "The silence was the loudest thing...", "Little did they know",
      isolated one-line fragments dropped in for drama ("Sixty thousand acres."). Allow at
      most ONE "what X did not know / did not yet know" suspense turn in the whole script.
   E. VARY sentence length and openers. Don't start consecutive sentences or paragraphs with
      the same word or the same subject-verb shape. Mix long flowing lines with short hard
      ones, but not on a mechanical repeating rhythm.
   Keep the strong COLD-OPEN hook and the warm, sincere TONE — only the mechanical tells go.
   (Reference de-slop pass: Story 10, 2026-08-11.)

These directives override any older guidance that conflicts.
