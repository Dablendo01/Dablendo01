LANTERN & DUST — HUMILIATION TITLE BANK (bent from African-tales outliers)
Research date: 2026 · Source niche: African folktales / emotional village stories.
That niche runs the SAME engine as ours (humiliated woman -> hidden truth ->
public reversal) at far larger scale. These titles bend its proven outliers into
the 1880s American Old West. Standing focus: STORIES ABOUT WOMEN'S HUMILIATION,
where the humiliated woman is the sympathetic underdog we root for (like Della).

THE 9 PROVEN TITLE PATTERNS (distilled)
=====================================================================
A. "They mocked her in [public place] — not knowing who she was" (hidden identity)
B. "Everyone mocked the [role] — until [event/object] changed everything"
C. "He invited the wife he cast off to mock her — she arrived in wealth" (wedding reversal)
D. "He made his wife a servant — so she took everything / left" (karma)
E. "They laughed when she married the [scorned] man — he owned an empire" (hidden-wealth spouse)
F. "Too poor / too plain / too ugly — until her secret changed everything"
G. "Her family sent her to menial work to hide her — she was found anyway"
H. "They humiliated the servant — until her real identity was revealed" (lost heiress)
I. "Public shaming -> one man's defiance / a stranger's bid"

WILD-WEST TRANSLATION KEY (for bending any African-tales title):
  billionaire/CEO      -> cattle baron / railroad owner / land baron / gold-strike man
  maid / housemaid     -> hired girl / scullery maid / washerwoman / kitchen girl
  village wife         -> mail-order bride / homestead wife / frontier wife
  the market / mall    -> the town square / general store / church social / barn dance
  the wedding          -> the church wedding / the barn dance / the town social
  private jet          -> private railroad car / fine black carriage / gold-trimmed coach
  removed her wig      -> let down her hair / lifted her veil / took off her mourning bonnet
  the company/inheritance -> the deed to the ranch / the whole valley / the railroad / gold claim
  prince / CEO son     -> the cattle baron's son / the marshal / the judge
  lost princess        -> the lost heiress / the rancher's stolen daughter
  wheelchair man       -> a one-legged veteran / a blind old rancher who owns the territory

NOTES
=====================================================================
- WOMAN AS SYMPATHETIC UNDERDOG, always. The African niche also runs "the woman
  is the cruel one who gets taught a lesson" (dirty-housewife type). We do NOT
  do that — our humiliated woman is the one we root FOR.
- Check canon-registry before naming; several of these engines resemble stories
  already made (Supper's Free = railroad reveal; Della = run out of town; $3 Bride
  = bought/mocked girl). Use them as a swipe pool, not duplicate premises.
- Keep titles 70-110 chars, em-dash pivot, withhold the payoff, one concrete image
  or number, max one ALL-CAPS word.
