LANTERN & DUST — BRAND VOICE & IDENTITY

=====================================================================
CHANNEL
=====================================================================
Name: Lantern & Dust
Tagline: "Cinematic tales from the American frontier."
About: Lantern & Dust tells cinematic, emotional fictional stories from the
American Old West (1860s–1890s) — love, loss, courage, kindness, and redemption
under open skies. New story every week.
Format: Faceless, narrated long-form (AI/stock cinematic visuals + voiceover).
Audience: Primarily US, age 45+, who watch for comfort, emotion, and escape.
Disclaimer stance: Stories are FICTION / dramatized. Never claim they are true history.

STANDING STORY FOCUS: a sympathetic WOMAN'S HUMILIATION -> endurance -> hidden
truth/protector -> public vindication. She is the underdog we root FOR, never the
cruel woman who "gets taught a lesson." (See standing-directives + humiliation-title-bank.)

=====================================================================
NARRATOR PERSONA
=====================================================================
A warm, weathered storyteller — like an old friend recounting a tale by firelight.
Calm, unhurried, deeply sincere. Never sensational or breathless. The voice
comforts even when the story aches. Think: a grandfather who has seen hard winters
and still believes in decency.

=====================================================================
TONE
=====================================================================
- Warm, cinematic, comforting, sincere, emotionally generous.
- Gentle gravity; earned sentiment, not melodrama.
- Hopeful core: kindness matters, the overlooked are seen, the shamed are vindicated.

=====================================================================
DO
=====================================================================
- Concrete sensory frontier detail: dust, lamplight, cold mornings, creaking
  porches, the smell of coffee, horses in the dark.
- Plain, dignified language. Strong verbs. Short sentences for tension.
- Root for the underdog; let small kindnesses carry weight.
- Let silences and small gestures do emotional work.

=====================================================================
DON'T
=====================================================================
- No cartoon cowboy slang ("howdy partner," "yeehaw," "reckon" overload).
- No graphic violence or gore; keep it family-safe and emotional, not brutal.
- No modern slang or anachronisms.
- No claiming true history; no real living people; no politics or religion debates.
- No melodrama that announces emotion ("it was so incredibly sad").
- Never make the humiliated woman the villain; she is always the one we root for.

=====================================================================
WORD/PHRASE PALETTE (favor)
=====================================================================
frontier, prairie, homestead, lantern, dusk, ridge, hollow, cabin, hearth,
widow, drifter, rancher, blacksmith, kindness, stranger, winter, mercy,
quiet, weathered, golden light, long shadows.

=====================================================================
VISUAL IDENTITY (for thumbnails/branding)
=====================================================================
Palette: warm dusk — burnt sienna, gold, charcoal, cream.
Logo motif: a single glowing oil lantern, dust in the light, dark background.

THUMBNAIL DOCTRINE — TEXT-FREE, MAXIMUM DRAMA + CLARITY (niche standard; never suggest overlay text)
- NO text overlay ever. The long descriptive title does the verbal work; the image
  carries the curiosity gap visually.
- ONE instantly readable, DRAMATIC emotional moment that makes a scrolling viewer
  need to click and then stay. The face is the click: a large, well-lit human FACE
  at its emotional peak — shame, defiance, stunned recognition, a mother's fury,
  pleading, tenderness.
- One clear action/story beat, nothing cluttered. Test at 168px wide: if you can't
  read the emotion in half a second, simplify.
- Warm-vs-cold light contrast is the house look: lamplight/firelight against snow,
  dusk, rain, or shadow.
- Consistent burnt-sienna/gold grade on EVERY thumbnail — with no text, the color
  grade is the channel's recognizable brand in the feed.
- The antagonist or a jeering crowd may appear, but must NEVER steal focus from the
  woman's face. No giant distracting foreground objects (hands, props) that read as
  a blob at small size.
- Audience is 45+, often on TV screens: a clean film-still reads premium; meme-style
  text reads cheap to this demographic.
- Photorealistic, high contrast, single focal subject, 16:9.
