Setting: Poplar Bend (flashback town: Ballard's Crossing)

STORY 8 — "The Empty Place" (Sweetwater) [the screamer]
Setting: Sweetwater
Characters: Winifred "Winnie" Holloway (lead, the waiting mother), Jonah Holloway
            (the stolen son, returns a circuit judge), Malcolm Radcliffe (antagonist
            banker), Thomas Holloway (dead husband, flashback), Effie Dell (faithful
            neighbor). Young Jonah (age 6) appears in flashback only.

STORY 9 — "The Town Mocked the Hired Girl Who Ate in the Barn" (Cottonwood)
Setting: Cottonwood valley
Characters: Clara Winslow (lead, hired girl/heir), Cordelia Tate (rich lonely widow/
            benefactor; frail variant Ingredient), Louisa Sennett (antagonist wife),
            Rufus Sennett (her husband), Judge Thaddeus Harlan (reads the will),
            Levi (young ranch hand ally, later her husband)

STORY 10 — "He Sat His Cast-Off Bride at the Back to Laugh" (Larkspur + Northern Range)
Setting: Larkspur (town) + the Northern Range
Characters: Josephine Ellery (lead, cast-off wife/secret heiress; stunned variant),
            Caleb Carraway (villain groom), Adelaide Vestry (second bride, redeemed),
            August Vestry (banker, her father), Marshal Isaiah Cobb (U.S. Marshal,
            reveal figure), Beatrice (boardinghouse widow ally). Uncle Augustus Ellery
            (dead, unseen — shares the Ellery surname by design as her blood kin).

STORY 11 — "She Can't Even Talk … Named the Killer in Ink" (Elk Bend)
Setting: Elk Bend
Characters: Verna Larkin (mute lead, laundry girl/witness), Hattie Bramble (murdered kind
            widow), Jasper Hargrove (killer, trusted deacon), Owen Prescott (schoolteacher
            protector), Sheriff Fenn (minor). Circuit judge (unnamed).

STORY 12 — "Build the Gallows Now … Before the Jury Ever Spoke" (Copper Hollow)
Setting: Copper Hollow
Characters: Adeline Whitmore (widow on trial, lead), Clement Sutter (dead attacker),
            Ambrose Sutter (antagonist, his rich father), Dr. Nathaniel Ashford (protector,
            the doctor), Opal Marsh (neighbor ally). Circuit judge (unnamed, outside authority).
