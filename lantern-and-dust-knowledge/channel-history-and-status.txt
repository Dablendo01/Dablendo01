4. PRODUCTION PIPELINE (repeat for every story)
=====================================================================
1. Premise from the women-humiliation focus; title from humiliation-title-bank /
   P1–P7 patterns.
2. Full script ~5,900–6,500 words => LOCKED TARGET 33–40 min (narrator ~155–177 wpm) with
   [SECTION] markers, (re-hook) every 2–3 min, mid-roll cliff, lantern CTA. Obey the
   INTRO-CLIFF doctrine (first 90s). Do NOT come in short like 9–14 did.
3. NO-MISTAKES PASS: timeline/number check; canon-registry name check; period sweep;
   intro-cliff check; filter-safety scan.
4. Character bible + direct portrait prompts (one Flow Ingredient per character;
   variant states = separate Ingredients).
5. Record VO -> send exact runtime.
6. VO-SYNCED shot list via scene_calculator.py: feed narration-only text (or the
   .srt for frame-exact) -> each image's duration = the narration under it. Author
   one prompt per scene, names inline, no em-dashes, text-free props, and STAMP THE
   PERIOD ANCHOR on every line (", 1880s American Old West frontier, period-accurate…
   no modern objects"). Audit for duplicates + filter risks.
7. Thumbnails: 3 text-free, cinematic TWO-HANDER concepts (woman + man across a charged
   gap, crowd watching); single-face only as a B-test. See brand-voice.txt.
8. SEO package: description, ~25 tags, 7 hashtags, pinned comment.
9. Cold-open animation prompts (scenes 1–15): motion-only over the stills.
10. Upload checklist: playlist slot #2, end-screen template, 2 cards (~40%/~85%),
    description footer, captions from script, pin the comment.

5. HARD RULES (do not violate)
=====================================================================
- CANON: never reuse a character first/surname or town from a past story (see
  canon-registry.txt, current through story 8). Avoid near-collisions.
- INTRO-CLIFF: person+injustice in sentence one; title promise by ~10s; stay-beat by
  60s; first shot is the person, not a wide.
- PERIOD ANCHOR on every image-prompt line (fixes modern-setting renders).
- VEO AUTOMATOR: names inline; no em-dashes inside prompt bodies.
- MINORS FILTER: never pair a child-word with a distress-word; peril on adults.
- DEATH SCENES: peaceful-sleep framing, closed coffins.
- TEXT-FREE applies to props too (no readable signs/engravings/ledgers).
- Woman is the sympathetic underdog we root FOR, never the villain "taught a lesson."
