1. CHARACTER BIBLE (5 named — each a Flow Ingredient)
=====================================================================
VERNA LARKIN — lead. 19. Mute since a childhood fever; the town's laundry girl, wrongly
  thought simple. Thin, quick dark watchful eyes, rough red hands, plain gray work dress.
  A brilliant silent watcher. The underdog we root for.
HATTIE BRAMBLE — the murdered kind widow. ~70. Sharp-tongued, soft-hearted; the only soul
  who ever treated Verna as whole. Owns the coveted well/land. Appears alive (Act 1) and
  the loss drives the story. (Death scene: peaceful framing, NO on-screen strangling
  detail — imply through the window/shadow only.)
JASPER HARGROVE — killer/antagonist. ~40. Handsome, charming, universally trusted church
  deacon; a patient cold snake beneath. Kills Hattie for her water. Charm cracks to fury.
OWEN PRESCOTT — protector. ~28. New Eastern schoolteacher, bookish, spectacles, ink-stained,
  mild but sharp. Teaches Verna to read/write — hands her the key to speak.
SHERIFF FENN — minor. ~50. Tired, slow, not evil; believes the man who can talk. In over
  his head.
(Circuit judge appears late, unnamed, as the outside authority.)

4. SEO — DESCRIPTION
=====================================================================
Verna Larkin had not spoken a word in eleven years — and the whole town of Elk Bend had
decided a girl who couldn't talk couldn't think. Simple, they called her. The mute girl who
did the washing. So when Verna crouched in the dark and watched a man she couldn't name close
his hands around the throat of the one person who was ever kind to her, she had no way on earth
to tell it. No scream. No voice. No words. The killer knew it — and he smiled at her over the
grave. But a killer, and a whole town, had made one mistake: they mistook a silent girl for an
empty one. This is the story of the autumn Elk Bend learned that some voices don't come out of
the mouth.
