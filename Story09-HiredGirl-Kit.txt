1. CHARACTER BIBLE (max 6 named — each a Flow Ingredient)
=====================================================================
CLARA WINSLOW — lead. 19. Orphaned hired girl. Plain gray work dress, white apron,
  brown hair pinned back simply, chapped hands. Quiet, honest, dignified, never
  complains; folds pain away small. The underdog we root for.
CORDELIA TATE — benefactor. ~78. Richest, loneliest widow in the valley; owns the
  4,000-acre Tate spread and the only sure water. Sharp, guarded, dry humor; trusts
  no one until Clara. Long white hair, fine but faded shawl, weathered proud face.
CORDELIA TATE (frail) — variant Ingredient: same woman, near death, wrapped in a
  shawl by a lamp at her writing desk, hand trembling over a pen.
LOUISA SENNETT — antagonist. ~40. Handsome, cold, socially ambitious wife who makes
  Clara eat in the barn. Soft cruel voice, careful clothes, front-pew airs. Mourning
  black in the will scene.
RUFUS SENNETT — antagonist (secondary). ~45. Louisa's husband; "took the girl in"
  for free labor. Good black coat, thumbs in his vest, self-satisfied.
JUDGE THADDEUS HARLAN — reveal figure. ~65. Territorial judge who reads the will.
  White whiskers, flat hard fair eyes, dark judicial coat, spectacles.
LEVI — ally. ~22. New young ranch hand who pulls out a chair for Clara; sees her
  worth before the money. Rough range clothes, open honest face; marries her later.

4. SEO — DESCRIPTION
=====================================================================
For two long winters, orphaned hired girl Clara Winslow carried her supper out to a
cold barn and ate alone with the horses while the family she served laughed in a warm
bright house — and the whole town of Cottonwood let it be. Clara never complained.
She rose before the sun, worked till dark, and gave thanks for the little she had.
The only soul who ever saw her worth was a lonely dying widow up on the pine road —
old Cordelia Tate, the richest woman in the valley, who had spent twenty years
watching for one honest heart. This is the story of the Saturday the will was read
in the Cottonwood courthouse... and the proud town found out exactly who the hired
girl had been all along.
