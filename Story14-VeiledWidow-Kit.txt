4. SEO — DESCRIPTION
=====================================================================
For six months, no one in Winter Ridge had seen the face of the widow who wore the veil. She
came to town all in mourning black, took a small room, and never once lifted that heavy veil
for a living soul — so the town, which could not abide a mystery, decided it hated her. They
said she was hiding a scarred face, a shameful secret, a proud contempt for honest folk. And
Millicent Prewitt, who ran the church committee and the town's cruelty with the same iron hand,
set out to drive her off. But not one sneering soul in Winter Ridge knew the truth: that half
of them owed the roof over their heads to the very woman they were tormenting. This is the story
of the Sunday that veil finally came up — and a whole town had to live with what it had done
under it.
