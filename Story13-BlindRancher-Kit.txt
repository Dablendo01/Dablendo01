4. SEO — DESCRIPTION
=====================================================================
The whole town of Juniper Flat laughed the day Susannah Truett agreed to marry the blind man.
She was thirty-one, plain, a spinster the town had long since set on the shelf; he was a blind
rancher on a hardscrabble spread everyone pitied. Two leftovers, they said. The plainest woman
in the valley and the sightless recluse — well, at least they'd have each other's misery. But
the town was laughing too hard to see the one thing Susannah had already seen. And a blind man
who cannot be fooled by a pretty face had seen something too: the only honest heart in Juniper
Flat. This is the story of the winter the laughing town learned it had gotten both of them
exactly backwards.
